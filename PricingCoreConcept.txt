Cumulocity SaaS Pricing Overview (Feb 2026)1. Core Concepts
Pricing Model: Usage-based, focused on customer data.
Contracting Model (Commit-to-Consume): Customers commit to a total spend amount (not quantities). Usage is metered daily and draws down the commitment. No Bill of Materials.
Auto-Renewal: If commitment depletes, a "top-up" commitment is applied. Contracts auto-renew (12-month term) if terms end without a new agreement.
Rollover: Unused commitment at contract expiration is forfeited (no carry-forward).
